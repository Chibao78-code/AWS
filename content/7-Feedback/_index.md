### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The First Cloud AI Journey program provided a professional, open, and motivating learning environment. I was encouraged to manage my own progress, research unfamiliar topics, and discuss problems when I needed another perspective. This balance between independence and support helped me become more disciplined and confident while working with cloud technologies. The technical sharing sessions and community activities also made the program more engaging and gave me opportunities to learn beyond the assigned materials.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**

I greatly appreciated the support from my mentor and the admin team throughout the internship. Instead of immediately providing a solution, my mentor often guided me with questions and suggestions so that I could identify the problem and verify the answer myself. This approach improved both my problem-solving ability and my sense of responsibility for the quality of my work. The admin team communicated important information clearly and provided timely assistance with the program's procedures and activities, allowing me to focus on learning and completing my report.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Major**

The program was closely related to my Software Engineering major. It allowed me to connect concepts from university, such as software architecture, networking, databases, security, and system development, with practical cloud use cases. Designing an IoT weather platform on AWS helped me think about requirements, scalability, security, and cost as parts of one solution. Building the workshop on secure hybrid access to Amazon S3 also gave me a clearer understanding of how networking and access-control decisions affect a real architecture.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

During the internship, I strengthened my knowledge of AWS services and tools, including the AWS Management Console, AWS CLI, Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, VPC, VPC endpoints, AWS PrivateLink, and IAM policies. Researching and writing three architecture blogs introduced me to additional topics such as Amazon EKS, protection against DDoS attacks with AWS WAF and CloudFront, and development environments for Agentic AI. More importantly, I improved my ability to read technical documentation, compare architectural options, estimate cost, test configurations, write bilingual documentation, and explain technical ideas in a structured way.

**5. Program Culture and Team Spirit**

The culture of the program encouraged knowledge sharing, respectful communication, and continuous improvement. Mentors, organizers, and community members were willing to share practical experience and help interns understand not only how an AWS service works, but also why a particular design choice is appropriate. This made me feel comfortable asking questions, receiving feedback, and revising my work. It also showed me the value of collaboration and clear communication when solving technical problems.

**6. Internship Program and Learning Activities**

The combination of self-study, hands-on practice, blog writing, events, and workshop development created a useful learning path from foundational knowledge to architectural thinking. I especially valued having concrete deliverables because they required me to turn what I had learned into something that could be reviewed and shared with others. For future cohorts, the experience could be improved further by publishing the schedule and expected outcomes of major milestones earlier, and by adding more group-based practical sessions in which interns can jointly design, deploy, troubleshoot, and review a small system.

### Additional Questions

**What did you enjoy the most during your internship?**

The most satisfying part was seeing my knowledge develop through actual deliverables. The IoT weather platform proposal helped me practice designing a serverless solution around a real problem; the three blog posts broadened my understanding of modern AWS architectures; and the S3 VPC endpoint workshop required me to organize a complete hands-on learning experience. These activities helped me move from simply learning individual services to understanding how services work together in a secure, scalable, and cost-conscious architecture.

**What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**

I believe future interns would benefit from more guided team exercises and technical review sessions. Examples could include building a small end-to-end system, troubleshooting a simulated incident, reviewing IAM and network configurations, or comparing different architectures based on security, reliability, and cost. A clear roadmap announced early in the program would also help interns plan their university workload and prepare more effectively for each milestone.

**Would you recommend this internship to your friends? Why?**

Yes, especially to students interested in cloud computing, software architecture, DevOps, or system engineering. The program combines independent study with practical assignments and support from experienced people. It not only develops AWS knowledge but also trains important professional skills such as research, technical writing, presentation, time management, and receiving feedback constructively.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**

I suggest adding a small team capstone near the end of the program. Each team could analyze a problem, propose an AWS architecture, deploy a minimum working version, apply security and cost controls, and present the lessons learned. Peer-review sessions between teams would make the experience more interactive and allow interns to learn from different approaches. It would also be helpful to share the dates of workshops, reviews, and other important activities as early and consistently as possible.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**

Yes. If given the opportunity, I would like to continue with more advanced topics in serverless architecture, DevOps and CI/CD, containers, IoT, cloud security, observability, and AWS cost optimization. I would also like to apply these skills to a larger project that can be deployed, monitored, evaluated, and improved over several iterations.

**Additional Comments**

I sincerely thank my mentor, the admin team, the speakers, and the First Cloud AI Journey community for their guidance and support throughout the internship. The program gave me a stronger technical foundation, a more systematic way of approaching cloud architecture, and a clearer direction for my future learning. I hope the program will continue to grow and provide meaningful opportunities for future interns.
